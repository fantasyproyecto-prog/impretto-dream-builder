# Impretto Home Landing

Act as an expert UI/UX designer and SEO frontend developer. Create a high-converting, modern, and responsive English landing page from scratch for a US-based bathroom remodeling business called "Impretto Home". The goal is to showcase the brand's premium essence, build immediate trust, and capture homeowner leads through a clean, conversion-focused design and strong on-page SEO structure.

Design & Tone:

Style: Modern, minimalist, elegant, and clean (think luxury interior design aesthetics). Use a sophisticated color palette (e.g., warm whites, deep charcoal/navy, and subtle metallic or earthy accent tones).

Typography: Crisp, legible sans-serif for headings and clean typography for body text.

UX: Mobile-first, fast-loading, with clear visual hierarchy and micro-interactions on hover.

Page Architecture & Content Structure (Section by Section):

Header & Navigation:

Clean Logo placeholder for "Impretto Home".

Sticky navigation bar with anchor links: Services, Portfolio, Why Us, Testimonials, Contact.

Prominent Call-to-Action (CTA) button: "Call Now" or "Free Estimate".

Hero Section (SEO & High Conversion):

H1 Tag: "Transform Your Space: Luxury Bathroom Remodeling in [City/State Area]"

Subheadline: "We bring elegance, functionality, and stress-free renovations to your home. Licensed, insured, and built to last."

Action Area: A quick 3-step Lead Capture Form directly on the hero (Fields: Name, Phone Number, ZIP Code, and a "Get My Free Quote" submit button).

Trust Badges: Visual icons for "Licensed & Insured", "10+ Years of Experience", and "100% Satisfaction Guarantee".

The "Why Choose Impretto Home" Section:

Focus on the brand's essence: attention to detail, transparent pricing, and clean work environments.

Use a 3-column grid highlighting: Custom Design, Premium Materials, and On-Time Completion.

Core Services Section (SEO Keyword Focused - H2 Tags):

Grid or cards detailing specific services: "Master Bathroom Renovations", "Custom Shower Conversions", "Modern Vanity & Tile Installations", and "Accessible Bathroom Upgrades".

Visual Proof / Portfolio (Interactive):

An interactive "Before & After" image comparison slider to showcase dramatic transformations.

A clean grid gallery of recent modern bathroom projects.

Our Proven 3-Step Process:

Step 1: Free In-Home Consultation & Design.

Step 2: Transparent Proposal & Material Selection.

Step 3: Precision Craftsmanship & Clean Delivery.

Social Proof & Local SEO Section:

3 star-rated customer reviews highlighting communication and quality (include customer first name and local city/neighborhood for local SEO relevance).

Final CTA Banner:

Headline: "Ready to Build the Bathroom of Your Dreams?"

Subtitle: "Schedule your complimentary design consultation today."

Large CTA Button: "Claim Your Free Estimate".

SEO Footer:

NAP Info: Business Name, Address/Service Area, Phone Number, and Email.

Areas We Serve: A dedicated keyword list of local service cities/counties.

Copyright and Privacy Policy links.

Technical Requirements: Ensure all headings (H1, H2, H3) follow strict SEO hierarchy. Include descriptive alt-text placeholders for all remodeling images. Make sure all forms have validation and buttons have clear focus states.
